# Maker Day 2 grading rubric (coach scores the team on four criteria and notes individual participation)

| Criterion | Strong | Adequate | Weak |
|---|---|---|---|
| Analysis | Covers the next best option, the net benefit and feasibility, including the financials. Claims have evidence. The team names its riskiest claim. | Covers most of the analysis. Some claims have no evidence. | Parts of the analysis are missing, or claims rest on belief alone. |
| Mockup | Shows the key parts of the product clearly. The value proposition is visible in it. | Shows the product, but key parts are missing or hard to follow. | No mockup, or a mockup that does not match the pitch. |
| Central business logic | Two clear sentences that follow from the analysis, and a wedge smaller than the vision. | The logic is stated but loosely tied to the analysis, or the wedge is vague. | No clear logic or wedge. |
| Execution and participation | Within time. Slides uploaded on time. Every member presented. The team paid attention throughout. | Minor overruns. Some members distracted at times. | Over time, late upload, or members absent or distracted during other teams' presentations. |
